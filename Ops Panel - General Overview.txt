It's a custom internal business management tool built on top of free/low-cost infrastructure instead of off-the-shelf software like HubSpot, QuickBooks, or FreshBooks.

The core idea: rather than paying for and adapting to a generic SaaS product, the business owns its own purpose-built panel that does exactly what it needs — no more, no less — with its data living in tools it already uses (Notion, Google Drive, Gmail).

More broadly, it's an example of a pattern that makes a lot of sense for small service businesses:

- Notion as the database — flexible, human-readable, easy to edit manually when needed
- Google Apps Script as the backend — runs on Google's infrastructure, no servers to manage, deeply integrated with Drive and Gmail out of the box
- A single HTML page as the frontend — served directly from the script, no hosting needed
- AI (Claude Vision) for the tedious parts — receipt extraction so logging an expense is a photo, not a data entry session

The result is something that behaves like a professional ops panel — clients, invoices, payments, contracts, reporting — but costs almost nothing to run, has no vendor lock-in on the core data, and can be changed by whoever built it whenever the business needs something different.

The tradeoff is that it requires technical effort to build and maintain, which makes it a good fit when either the business has that capacity in-house or works closely with someone who does.
